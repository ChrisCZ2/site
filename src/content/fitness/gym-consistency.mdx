## The split

Most weeks I aim for **3–4 sessions** — mix of strength and cardio. Bug hunting and PortSwigger labs sit on a separate mental track; the gym is the reset.

## What works

- **Fixed days** beat motivation. Same slots after work or before deep focus blocks.
- **Short sessions** on heavy lab weeks (45 min) beat skipping entirely.
- **Protein and sleep** matter as much as PRs when you are staring at Burp for hours.

Security is the craft; fitness is the maintenance. Both get writeups here — different folders, same discipline.
